How to play

Each puzzle is a real match and its real starting lineup. Tap a shirt and type the name the player is known by: usually the surname (Vardy, Van Dijk, Tadić), or the one name for players known by one (Xavi, Neymar). Each dash is one letter; hyphens and spaces in a name are shown but not typed.

6 tries per player. Hard gives 4 tries and hides the flags. Skip accents and spaces: č/ć = C, š = S, ž = Z, đ = DJ, Van Dijk = VANDIJK.
